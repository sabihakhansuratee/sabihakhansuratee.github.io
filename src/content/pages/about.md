I'm a marketing communications professional based in Singapore, with over five years'
experience across education, healthcare, and B2B technology. I currently lead
marketing communications for the Centre of Regulatory Excellence (CoRE) at Duke-NUS
Medical School, where I design collateral and social content that promotes the
centre's work to a global regulatory, academic, and industry audience.

Day to day, that means running the full communications cycle for a high-volume events
programme — 14 events a year, reaching up to 1,000 attendees from across the world.
I own audience outreach and registration drives, produce the full range of collateral
each event needs — flyers, banners, signage, decks, programme booklets, and
certificates — and cover events live across LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, and our
website, before writing up what happened afterwards. I also write across formats
beyond events: landing and event pages, a weekly newsletter to 5,000 recipients,
event reports, and publications, and I design and produce most visual assets
in-house, which shortens turnaround and keeps a consistent look across everything the
centre puts out.

Before Duke-NUS, I ran corporate social media and content for Fujifilm Business
Innovation Singapore, and before that, designed multimedia learning content at
Singapore Polytechnic. Across all three roles, the common thread has been taking
something technical or unfamiliar and making it clear, visual, and easy for the right
audience to act on.

I'm bilingual in English and Bahasa Melayu, and hold a First Class Honours degree in
Business and Marketing from Coventry University, alongside certificates in
promotional writing and social media content from Singapore Management University.
