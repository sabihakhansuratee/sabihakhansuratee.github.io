The launch cohort of the Halyard Regulatory Fellows Programme needed a small, coherent
set of materials in under two weeks: a recruitment flyer, a certificate template for
graduating fellows, and a short announcement for social media once places were filled.

The flyer kept to one clear message — who the programme was for, and how to apply —
so it could be read at a glance and forwarded easily by faculty. The certificate used
the same type and colour treatment as the flyer, so the programme read as one thing
across its whole lifecycle rather than a new look at every stage.

The announcement post went out once applications closed, thanking applicants and
naming the cohort size without singling anyone out before formal offers went out.

Nothing here needed a large team or a long runway — the brief was to make a first-year
programme look established from day one, on a modest production budget.
