Not every event can be shown the way it happened. The Solstice Regulatory Roundtable
is closed-door by design — the discussion, the attendee list, and the working
documents used in the room are confidential, and none of that belongs on a public
portfolio regardless of how the design work turned out.

What's shown here instead is the part that was always meant to be public: the outreach
that built the room, and the recap that went out once it closed. A single flyer
announced that the roundtable was happening and who could request an invitation,
without naming attendees in advance. The international and senior-level reach of the
eventual attendee list is real, even though no attendee is named.

Afterwards, a short LinkedIn post marked that the roundtable had taken place and
thanked participants collectively, again without singling anyone out or describing
what was discussed.

No results are tracked for this kind of work — a closed-door roundtable isn't measured
the way an open conference is, and inventing a number here would suggest otherwise.
